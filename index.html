02 · Rose Varieties

Six roses, one red soil.

Our founders scouted genetics at the world's leading flower trade shows before choosing the six varieties that now thrive across Tribloom's greenhouses.

01

Taj Mahal

A monument to love, in bloom.

Full, generous, blush-pink petals that unfurl slowly and hold their shape for days — a rose named for timelessness itself.

02

Avalanche Rose

The world's favourite white.

Ivory petals brushed with the faintest blush — the industry benchmark for weddings, ceremonies, and grand arrangements.

Planted 24.05.2026
5,128 plants · 23 beds
03

Sweet Avalanche

Softer, rosier, ours.

A gentler take on the Avalanche family, in soft rose-pink — one of the largest plantings on our farm today, straight from our own grow sheets.

04

Peach Avalanche

Warm as a Karnataka evening.

Apricot and peach tones that glow under the greenhouse light — a quiet, romantic favourite among florists.

05

Jumilia Rose

Cream, blushed in coral.

Elegant and full-bodied, with a soft coral edge that deepens as the bloom matures — a florist's favourite for statement arrangements.

Planted 01.06.2026
1,222 plants · 5 beds
06

Revival Rose

Renewal, petal by petal.

A soft, romantic pink that lives up to its name — one of our newest beds, already thriving in Tribloom's red earth.

Bed counts and planting dates are pulled straight from our greenhouse tags — the same way we track every rose on the farm.

Our Founder

Shipra Sharma

Founding Director

A Quality Control expert with ten years of corporate experience — including a significant tenure at IBM — Shipra brings the rigour of the boardroom to the red soil of Karnataka. She holds an MCA from Banasthali Vidyapeeth.

Specialising in leveraging technology for social good, she has collaborated with BIT Mesra on soil health and geospatial research — pairing data-driven science with a founder's love for the land, so that every bloom Tribloom grows meets the same standard she once held complex systems to.
